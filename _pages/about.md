Contact: ana.fernandez-baranda@polytechnique.edu

I'm a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at [CMAP, Ecole Polytechnique](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/), as part of the [PhD track](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/phd-track/phd-track-mathematics) program of Institut Polytechnique de Paris. I work under the supervision of Sylvie Méléard and Vincent Bansaye. Since September 2025, my research has been supported by the [ERC project SINGER](https://www.ip-paris.fr/erc-singer-stochastic-dynamics-single-cells-growth-emergence-and-resistance). I am part of the group [PEIPS](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/peips) (Population Evolution and Interacting Particle Systems), the [Inria project team MERGE](https://team.inria.fr/merge/fr/) (Mathematics for Evolution, Reproduction, Growth and Emergence), as well as a member of the [Chaire MMB](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~chaire-mmb//index.html) (Chaire Modélisation Mathématique et Biodiversité). 

Research
======
I am an applied mathematician with a strong interest in application to biological phenomena. My doctoral work focuses on stochastic modeling of hematopoiesis (process by which blood cells are produced), and statistical analysis of clinical data to better understand Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (type of blood cancers). 

**Some keywords:**
-Stochastic processes

-Population dynamics

-Mixed effects models

-Mathematical biology
