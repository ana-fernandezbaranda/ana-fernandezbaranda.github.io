Contact: ana.fernandez-baranda[at]polytechnique[dot]edu

I'm a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at CMAP, Ecole Polytechnique as a part of the [PhD track](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/phd-track/phd-track-mathematics) program of Institut Polytechnique de Paris and since September 2025 funded by the [ERC project SINGER](https://www.ip-paris.fr/erc-singer-stochastic-dynamics-single-cells-growth-emergence-and-resistance). I work under the supervision of Sylvie Méléard and Vincent Bansaye. I am part of the group [PEIPS](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/peips) (Population Evolution and Interacting Particle Systems), the [Inria project team MERGE](https://team.inria.fr/merge/fr/) (Mathematics for Evolution, Reproduction, Growth and Emergence) as well as a member of the [Chaire MMB](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~chaire-mmb//index.html) (Chaire Modélisation Mathématique et Biodiversité).
